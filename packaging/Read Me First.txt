T3 for WoW — read me first
==========================

1. Move "T3 for WoW" into your Applications folder.
2. Open it. macOS will say it cannot verify the developer, because this free app is not notarized.
   Click Done, then go to System Settings > Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway" (within
   about an hour), and confirm with your password.
3. Click "T3" in the menu bar and follow its one next step:
   Connect T3 (approve on T3's own page) > Install Addon > Check Capture Access > start the game.
4. In World of Warcraft: Forever, type /t3

You need: an Apple silicon Mac with macOS 14+, World of Warcraft: Forever, and your own T3.
Nothing is shared with anyone else; see docs/PRIVACY.md in the source.
